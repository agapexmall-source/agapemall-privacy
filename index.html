Privacy Policy

Effective: 13 September 2026 · Version 2026-09-13

1. Information we collect

We may collect account information such as your username and email address; marketplace information such as listings, searches, favourites, offers, messages and reviews; verification information when you choose to verify your account; and technical information such as app version, device model, operating system, device locale, IP address and approximate location derived from your IP address.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, provide marketplace features, show relevant local content, support communication between buyers and sellers, process verification requests, prevent abuse and fraud, troubleshoot the service, provide customer support, send service notifications and improve Agape e-Mall.

3. Location and device information

Agape e-Mall may use device locale information and IP-based location information to determine an approximate country, region or city and to improve local marketplace results. IP-based location is not the same as precise GPS location. Device and app metadata may also be stored for security, compatibility and troubleshooting.

4. Service providers and sharing

We may use service providers that help us operate the app, including cloud hosting, authentication, databases, notifications, analytics and IP-based location services. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transfer. We do not treat your personal information as something to be sold merely for payment.

5. Marketplace visibility

Information you intentionally publish in a listing, seller profile, review or other public marketplace area may be visible to other users. Messages and offers may be visible to the users participating in that conversation or transaction and to authorised administrators where needed for safety, support or dispute handling.

6. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes and meet legal or operational requirements. Some records may remain for a limited period after account deletion where retention is necessary for fraud prevention, legal compliance, backups or dispute resolution.

7. Your choices and rights

You can review or update certain profile information in the app and can use the available account controls to request account deletion. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of certain personal information. Contact support if you need assistance with a privacy request.

8. Notifications and optional permissions

Notification permission is requested separately by your device. You can refuse or later disable notifications in your device or app settings. Optional permissions should not be treated as acceptance of this Privacy Policy.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information. No internet or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so users should also protect their passwords and devices.

10. Children

Agape e-Mall is not designed for children to create marketplace accounts or conduct transactions independently. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact support so the matter can be reviewed.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service changes. When a material update requires renewed acknowledgement, Agape e-Mall may ask you to review and acknowledge the new version before continuing to use account features.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact info@theagapemall.com.
